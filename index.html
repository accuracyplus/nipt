What is NIPT?

Safe answers, earlier in pregnancy

A simple maternal blood test from Week 10. It analyses fragments of the baby's DNA naturally circulating in the mother's bloodstream — providing vital insights with no risk to mother or baby.

Advanced Technology

Precision you can rely on

Our NIPT utilises the latest cell-free DNA sequencing technology, analysed in CAP-accredited and EIAC ISO 15189:2022 certified laboratories — delivering over 99% detection accuracy for the most common chromosomal conditions.

Safety

Zero Risk to Mother or Baby

Major medical organisations including ACOG and ACMG recommend prenatal screening for all pregnant women, regardless of age or individual risk factors.

  • Simple Blood Draw Only

    Less than 5 ml of maternal blood. No needles near the baby, no risk of miscarriage.

  • Pre-Test Counselling

    Our team ensures every patient understands the scope, limitations, and next steps before proceeding.

  • Post-Result Support

    Positive or inconclusive results are followed up with OB-GYN specialist coordination and further guidance.

  • Strictly Confidential

    Results shared only with the patient. Sharing with third parties requires your written consent.

Who should consider NIPT?

  • All pregnant women — screening is universally recommended
  • Women aged 35 or older, at higher baseline risk
  • Those with a personal or family history of chromosomal conditions
  • Women with an abnormal first-trimester ultrasound result
  • Anyone seeking early, accurate reassurance without procedural risk

FAQ

Your Questions, Answered.

NIPT (Non-Invasive Prenatal Testing) is a safe, advanced screening test used early in pregnancy to check for certain chromosomal conditions. It analyses small fragments of the baby's DNA naturally present in the mother's blood — no risk to the baby.

From Week 10 of pregnancy onward, up to Week 24. Weeks 10–14 are the ideal window, allowing results to be combined with your first-trimester ultrasound. Earlier testing gives earlier reassurance and more time for informed decision-making.

Completely safe. NIPT requires only a standard blood draw from the mother. Unlike amniocentesis or CVS, there is no needle near the baby and no risk of miscarriage or pregnancy complications.

Basic NIPT screens for Trisomy 21 (Down syndrome), Trisomy 18 (Edwards), Trisomy 13 (Patau), and key sex chromosome conditions. Advanced NIPT extends coverage to all 24 chromosomes, plus 6 microdeletion syndromes, and includes fetal gender as standard.

NIPT is a screening test — not a diagnostic test. It does not detect every possible condition. It cannot identify structural defects such as neural tube defects, rare single-gene mutations, or complications arising later in pregnancy. A positive result must be confirmed by a clinician before any action is taken.

Results are available within 7–10 working days from sample receipt. Every report is reviewed by a specialist before delivery. Our team will contact you directly once results are ready.
